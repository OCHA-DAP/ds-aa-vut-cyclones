Framework

The trigger mechanism is set out in section III.2 (Triggers and thresholds) of the National Anticipatory Action Framework for Tropical Cyclones in Vanuatu:

This framework will be triggered by a tropical cyclone forecasted or observed to expose at least 15,000 people to winds of at least 64 knots (approximately 119 km/h):

  • Readiness trigger: the RSMC Nadi forecast indicates that at least 15,000 people in Sanma, Shefa or Tafea are expected to be exposed to winds of at least 64 knots, 120 to 72 hours ahead.
  • Action trigger: the VMHS forecast indicates that at least 15,000 people in Sanma, Shefa or Tafea are expected to be exposed to winds of at least 64 knots, 72 to 24 hours ahead. Anticipatory activities should be completed before the operational cut-off of 24 hours before landfall.
  • Observational trigger: the VMHS observed track indicates that at least 15,000 people anywhere in Vanuatu have been exposed to winds of at least 64 knots. Activities under this trigger start after landfall and NDMO's all-clear bulletin.

Every time RSMC Nadi or VMHS shares a tropical cyclone track forecast, it is evaluated by an automated script to determine whether the trigger conditions have been met. Lead times are counted from the time the forecast was produced: the readiness value uses the part of the RSMC Nadi forecast track 72 to 120 hours ahead, and the action value the part of the VMHS forecast track 24 to 72 hours ahead. The observed track is made of the analysis (current) positions of all VMHS forecasts received for the cyclone. Population: WorldPop 2026, 1 km.
