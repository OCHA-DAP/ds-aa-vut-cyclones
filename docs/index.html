Vanuatu anticipatory action: tropical cyclones

The trigger of the National Anticipatory Action Framework for Tropical Cyclones in Vanuatu, endorsed in October 2026 for the 2026/27 and 2027/28 cyclone seasons: at least 15,000 people exposed to 64-knot winds, in the RSMC Nadi and VMHS forecasts or the VMHS observed track.
